## About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the **Future Technology Program (Robotics Track)** at **Guohao College, Tongji University**. I am interested in turning AI methods into real engineering systems—from problem definition and algorithm prototyping to simulation, system integration, and practical deployment.

I ranked in the **top 10% of my major** in my first year and was named a **Tongji University “Mingde Star” for the 2025–2026 academic year**. During my first year, I received eight university-level, one provincial-level, and four national-level competition awards. I am currently the first author or co-first author of a submitted paper in AI for Energy.

I maintain over 30 projects on GitHub. My work has primarily involved intelligent transportation and autonomous-driving safety, smart-campus digital twins, computer vision, and robotic systems. Beyond isolated benchmark metrics, I care about whether a complete system can run reliably, be clearly evaluated, and evolve through iteration.

I also operate more than one hundred online student communities reaching several thousand students, contributing to student communication and information sharing at Tongji University.

## Research Interests

* **Engineering Intelligence Systems**: Computer vision, time-series analysis, decision support, and deployable prototypes
* **Intelligent Transportation & Safety**: Driver-state monitoring, traffic situational awareness, and real-time alerts
* **Digital Twins & Simulation**: Smart-campus scenarios, 3D modeling, interactive interfaces, and engineering visualization
* **Robotics & Embodied Intelligence**: Robot perception, simulation platforms, and hardware-software co-design
* **AI for Science / Engineering**: Data analysis and intelligent modeling for energy and engineering problems
