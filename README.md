## About / 关于我

我目前就读于**同济大学国豪书院未来技术班（机器人方向）**，主要关注**工程智能**：把感知算法、数据分析、仿真平台和工程系统连接起来，做能够在真实场景中运行、验证和迭代的 AI 原型。

I am an undergraduate student in the **Future Technology Program (Robotics)** at **Guohao College, Tongji University**. My main interest is **Engineering Intelligence**: connecting perception algorithms, data analysis, simulation platforms, and deployable engineering systems into usable AI prototypes.

我的项目经历集中在**智能交通 / 自动驾驶安全、智慧校园数字孪生、机器人与具身智能、工程数据分析**等方向。我更希望把自己训练成一个能从问题定义、原型开发到系统集成持续推进的工程型研究者。

My project experience spans **intelligent transportation, autonomous-driving safety, smart-campus digital twins, robotics / embodied AI, and engineering data analysis**. I am working toward becoming a builder who can move from problem definition to prototype development and system integration.


## Focus / 关注方向

- **Engineering AI Systems / 工程智能系统**: video perception, time-series analysis, decision support, and deployable prototypes.
- **Intelligent Transportation & Safety / 智能交通与安全**: driver monitoring, low-altitude / traffic situation recognition, real-time warning pipelines.
- **Digital Twin & Simulation / 数字孪生与仿真**: smart-campus scenarios, Blender / Unity / MATLAB workflows, infrastructure-oriented visualization.
- **Robotics & Embodied AI / 机器人与具身智能**: VLA-related applications, Isaac Sim / OpenLoong exploration, robot perception and manipulation prototypes.
- **AI for Engineering Research / 面向工程科研的 AI**: computer vision, CNN / RNN time-series modeling, energy and materials analysis.
